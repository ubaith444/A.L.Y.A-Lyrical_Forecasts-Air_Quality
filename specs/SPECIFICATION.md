# A.L.Y.A — Project Feature Specification

## App Identity
- **App Name**: Alya Weather
- **Subtitle**: Lyrical Forecasts & Air Quality
- **Primary Market**: India
- **Primary Focus**: Weather forecasting, air quality monitoring, and climate intelligence.
- **Dashboard Language**: English
- **Target Platforms**: Responsive web application; mobile-friendly design.

## Curated Taglines
1. *The sky, simplified.* — Minimalist and clean.
2. *Clarity from the heavens.* — Elegant and atmospheric.
3. *Your daily breath of fresh air.* — Lifestyle and air quality focused.
4. *Atmosphere in harmony.* — Calm, fluid, and immersive.

---

## Architectural Environment Overview
- **Frontend Architecture**: Responsive web application designed with modern standards (mobile-friendly, clean light theme, accessible tokens).
- **Backend Architecture**: Scalable climate intelligence API (FastAPI/Python microservice) providing forecast aggregation and air quality processing.
- **Data Integration Scope**:
  - Pan-India weather forecasting (temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity, UV index).
  - Indian Air Quality Index (AQI) monitoring with pollutant metrics (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, O3, CO, SO2).
  - Lyrical forecast synthesis and climate intelligence insights.
